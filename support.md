# Break a Leg: Support

Need help with Break a Leg? Email **developer@gearheartai.org** and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?** No. Break a Leg has no sign-in. Everything stays on your iPad.

**How do I add a script?** On the Shelf, tap +, then Scan Pages, Open a PDF, or Paste Text. Check any
lines the app flags, then pick your child's part.

**My child is stuck on a line.** Tap Peek to show the line or Hear My Line to hear it, then tap Done.

**How do I buy more scripts or restore purchases?** Tap the gear on the Shelf, answer the grown-up
question, and open the Store or tap Restore Purchases. Purchases work with Family Sharing.

**How do I delete a script?** Tap the gear, answer the grown-up question, and choose the script to delete.
Deleting a script frees its slot.

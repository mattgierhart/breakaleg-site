Break a Leg: Line Rehearsal · Last updated: November 27, 2026

Break a Leg is a line rehearsal app for iPad made by Gearheart. It is built for families and young actors. Scripts you add yourself never leave your iPad.

The short version: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app collects nothing about you or your child. A show code fetches one script and we keep no IP addresses or device details.

What we collect

Nothing about you or your child. Break a Leg has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only time the app talks to us is when you use a show code, described next.

Show codes

A director can share a show with the cast using a show code. When you open a show link or type a code, the app asks our website for that one script. The request carries the code and nothing about you: no name, no account, no device ID. We count how many times each show is downloaded, so we know a show is being used. We keep no IP addresses and no device details, and we do not set cookies. Scripts you add yourself, by scanning, opening a PDF or pasting text, are never sent to us.

What stays on your iPad

  • Scripts. The text of scripts you add is stored on your iPad so you can rehearse. You can delete a script at any time from Grown-ups.
  • Scanned pages. When you scan pages with the camera, the app reads the text on the device. Each page photo is kept only until you have checked that page, then it is deleted. PDFs you open are read and not kept.
  • Progress. Stars, the part your child plays, voice choices and settings are stored on your iPad.

If your iPad is backed up to iCloud or a computer, this data is included in that backup under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan script pages, and only when you tap Scan Pages.

Purchases

Purchases in the app are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Purchases and settings are behind a grown-up check.

Cast Packs

A Cast Pack is bought by a director on this website, not in the app. Payment is handled by Stripe. We receive the director's name, email address and whether the payment went through. We do not receive card numbers. The script a director emails us is used only to set up that show.

Children

Break a Leg is made for children to use with a parent or teacher. The app has no accounts and sends nothing about your child, so we collect no personal information from children. Cast Packs are bought by directors and teachers, who are adults.

This website

This website has no cookies, no forms and no advertising or tracking scripts. To count visits to break-a-leg.app we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, does not fingerprint your browser and does not identify you; we see only totals such as page views, referring sites and countries. Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages may briefly process technical details such as your IP address to send the page to your browser, and the same is true when the app fetches a show code. Our own code does not store them, and we do not use them to identify you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Break a Leg: Line Rehearsal · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Break a Leg is a line rehearsal app for iPad made by Gearheart. It is built for families and young actors, and it is designed so that nothing about you or your child leaves your iPad.

The short version: we collect nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Break a Leg has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run a server for the app, so there is nowhere for your information to go.

What stays on your iPad

  • Scripts. The text of scripts you add is stored on your iPad so you can rehearse. You can delete a script at any time from Grown-ups.
  • Scanned pages. When you scan pages with the camera, the app reads the text on the device. Each page photo is kept only until you have checked that page, then it is deleted. PDFs you open are read and not kept.
  • Progress. Stars, the part your child plays, voice choices and settings are stored on your iPad.

If your iPad is backed up to iCloud or a computer, this data is included in that backup under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan script pages, and only when you tap Scan Pages.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Purchases and settings are behind a grown-up check.

Children

Break a Leg is made for children to use with a parent or teacher. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

This website

This website has no cookies, no forms and no advertising or tracking scripts. To count visits to break-a-leg.app we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, does not fingerprint your browser and does not identify you; we see only totals such as page views, referring sites and countries. Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages may briefly process technical details such as your IP address to send the page to your browser; we do not use them to identify you. The app itself collects nothing (see above).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

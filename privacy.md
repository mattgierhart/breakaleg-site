# Break a Leg: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 14, 2026_

Break a Leg is a line rehearsal app for iPad made by Gearheart. It is built for families and young actors,
and it is designed so that nothing about you or your child leaves your iPad.

## What we collect

Nothing. Break a Leg has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run a server
for the app, so there is nowhere for your information to go.

## What stays on your iPad

- **Scripts.** The text of scripts you add is stored on your iPad so you can rehearse. You can delete a
  script at any time from Grown-ups.
- **Scanned pages.** When you scan pages with the camera, the app reads the text on the device. Each page
  photo is kept only until you have checked that page, then it is deleted. PDFs you open are read and not
  kept.
- **Progress.** Stars, the part your child plays, voice choices and settings are stored on your iPad.

If your iPad is backed up to iCloud or a computer, this data is included in that backup under Apple's
terms. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

## Camera

The camera is used only to scan script pages, and only when you tap Scan Pages.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Purchases
and settings are behind a grown-up check.

## Children

Break a Leg is made for children to use with a parent or teacher. Because we collect no personal
information from anyone, we collect none from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

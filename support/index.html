Need help with Break a Leg? Email developer@gearheartai.org and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Break a Leg has no sign-in. Everything stays on your iPad.

How do I add a script?

On the Shelf, tap +, then Scan Pages, Open a PDF, or Paste Text. Check any lines the app flags, then pick your child's part.

Can we try it before adding our own script?

Yes. On the empty Shelf, tap "Or try the sample play, The Lighthouse Cat", pick a part, and tap Rehearse.

My child is stuck on a line.

Tap Peek to show the line or Hear My Line to hear it, then tap Done.

How do I buy more scripts or restore purchases?

Tap the gear on the Shelf, answer the grown-up question, and open the Store or tap Restore Purchases. Purchases work with Family Sharing.

How do I delete a script?

Tap the gear, answer the grown-up question, and choose the script to delete. Deleting a script frees its slot.

What does Break a Leg do with our data?

Nothing leaves your iPad. See the privacy policy.

Still stuck?

Email developer@gearheartai.org. It helps to tell us your iPad model, the iPadOS version, and what you tapped just before the problem.

How it works

Script in. Scene partner out.

Bring the pages home from rehearsal. In a few minutes your child is practicing with a full cast.

1Add the script

Scan the pages with the iPad camera, open a PDF, or paste the text. Break a Leg finds every character and line.

2Pick your part

Your child taps who they're playing. Every other character gets a voice of their own.

3Rehearse

The cast reads the scene. A chime plays on your child's turn and their line lights up. They say it, then tap Done.

Questions from parents

More answers on the support page.

Does it work with any script?

Yes. Scan printed pages, open a PDF, or paste the text. If a line is unclear, the app asks you to check it before rehearsing.

Do we need an account or internet?

No account. Everything happens on the iPad, and voices use the ones already installed on it.

What does it cost?

Your first script is included and the sample play is free. Add more scripts one at a time or unlock unlimited scripts, all with Family Sharing.

Which devices does it run on?

iPad with iPadOS 26 or later.

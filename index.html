How it works

From script pages to stage-ready in minutes

No more waiting for a grown-up to read every other part. Bring the script home from rehearsal and practice whenever your child is ready.

1

Add the script

Scan the pages from rehearsal with the iPad camera, open a PDF, or paste the text. Break a Leg finds the characters and their lines.

2

Pick your part

Your child chooses who they're playing. Every other character gets a voice of their own.

3

Rehearse

The cast reads the scene. When it's your child's turn, a chime plays and their line lights up in orange. They say it out loud and tap Done.

Made for young actors

Big buttons, clear cues

  • A clear "Your turn!" cue with no time limit
  • Peek or Hear My Line when they get stuck
  • Learn in steps: full lines, then first letters, then from memory
  • Earn up to three stars for every scene
  • Add feelings like Brave or Silly to practice expression
